# Evergreen vs Trend-Based Topics
*How to choose the twist hazard and setting for each series so the back catalogue keeps earning while new series ride seasonal search spikes.*

## Definitions
- **Evergreen:** the real hazard is always relevant (CO poisoning, hypothermia, drowning myths, heat stroke). Views compound for years.
- **Seasonal:** evergreen hazard with a yearly search spike (winter storms, hurricane season, summer heat, wildfire season).
- **Trend:** tied to a news event or a format trend. Big spike, short life.

## Target mix
| Type | Share of series | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Evergreen core | 60% | Long-tail search + suggested traffic |
| Seasonal timing | 30% | Release 2–4 weeks **before** the season peaks |
| Trend | ≤ 10% | Only if it fits the format in < 1 week of production |

## Seasonal calendar (Northern Hemisphere, adjust for the Spanish/Portuguese channels)
| Months | Hazard angles | Example setting |
|---|---|---|
| Nov–Feb | CO from generators/heaters, hypothermia, thin ice | Snowed-in cabin (S01) |
| Jun–Nov | Hurricane generator CO, flood water myths | Louisiana basement (S02 sting) |
| Jun–Aug | Heat stroke, rip currents, flash floods | Desert road trip, beach |
| Jul–Oct | Wildfire smoke, evacuation | Mountain town |
| Oct | Horror-season boost for the whole niche | Any. Publish a strong Part 1 |

## Scoring a topic (1–5 each, keep ≥ 18/25)
1. **Evergreen value:** still searched in 3 years?
2. **Visual twist:** can a flat cartoon show the clues?
3. **Safety value:** does the resolution teach something useful?
4. **Data strength:** Tier A sources exist (see Fact-Checking SOP)?
5. **Timing:** can it be released before its seasonal peak?

## Checking demand (free)
- Google Trends: compare the hazard term over 5 years, look at the seasonal curve.
- YouTube search autocomplete: type the hazard + "story", "what happens", "why".
- `yt-dlp` search pull (PROMPT LIBRARY → Research) for view counts of existing videos on the topic.

## Using trends without losing evergreen value
- Keep titles evergreen ("If Your Generator Hums All Night…") and put the timely angle in the description/pinned comment.
- Re-promote seasonal series every year: community post + a new Short + updated end screens.
- The full-series cut (25 min movie) is the evergreen asset to push each season.
