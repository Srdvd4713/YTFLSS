# High-Retention Niches & Series Backlog
*The scored niche decision lives in `Competitor Channel Breakdown/Channel Research & Niche Scoring - 2026-10-24.md` (winner: serialized survival + rules + real-science twist, 67/80). This file turns that into a backlog of future series and explains what makes a niche high-retention.*

## Why the chosen niche retains
- **Serial structure:** each Part ends open → next-Part clicks and session time.
- **Day counter/HUD:** a progress bar the viewer wants to see finish.
- **Clues:** rewatches and longer average view duration.
- **Twist + real science:** the payoff is shareable and advertiser-safe.

## High-retention traits checklist (any new niche or spin-off)
- [ ] Clear stakes in 10 s
- [ ] Recurring visual system (HUD, rule cards) viewers learn
- [ ] Natural Part breaks every 3–8 min
- [ ] A reveal that recontextualises earlier footage
- [ ] Can be produced in the existing render engine (no stock footage)

## Spin-off formats (low cost, same assets)
- **Explainer Shorts:** 30–45 s, one real fact per Short (3/week).
- **Clue compilations:** Part 7 per series.
- **Full-series movie cut:** all Parts stitched, ~25 min.
- **Podcastfy episode:** 15–20 min audio on the real hazard.

## When to pivot or add a niche
Only after 3 series. If average view % stays under ~35% and Part 1 → Part 2 click-through is weak across all three, revisit the scoring table and test niche B (rule-horror one-offs) with the same engine.
