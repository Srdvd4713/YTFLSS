# Audience Psychology Guide (Curiosity + Emotion)
*Why our viewers click, stay and comment, and how each production choice uses that. Audience definition: `CHANNEL BIBLE.md → Target audience`.*

## The 5 drivers
| Driver | What the viewer feels | How we trigger it | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Curiosity gap** | "I need to know what the lights are" | Specific unanswered question in title + thumbnail + line 1 | Final Sight / Bradikinin titles: 837K–2.7M views |
| **Dramatic irony** | "I see it, he doesn't. Turn around!" | HUD `AIR INSIDE: SAFE ✓` while symptoms rise; clues in plain sight | Twist-reversal comments are the top comments in the niche |
| **Safe dread** | Fear with a guaranteed landing | Cartoon style, deadpan narrator, a resolution every series | "last video before bed" comments |
| **Puzzle solving** | "I figured it out before Part 5" | 12 fair clues, Part 7 clue compilation | Clue hunting drives rewatches |
| **Moral debate** | "I would have opened the door" | Binary end-card question | Comment volume |

## Emotion map per series
Unease (P1) → dread (P2) → fear for the weak (P3, the dog) → conflict between siblings (P4) → shock + guilt (P5) → relief + learning (P6). Each Part should have **one dominant emotion**; write it at the top of `parts/partN.json` notes.

## Character empathy rules
- The narrator is **competent but wrong**: viewers respect him and feel the irony. Never make him stupid.
- A vulnerable character (child, pet) raises stakes without gore.
- The "threat" is secretly the protector (rescuers, the deer). This reversal is what the audience shares.

## What breaks trust (avoid)
- Clickbait the video doesn't pay off (thumbnail figure never appears).
- An unfair twist (no clues planted).
- Real gore or real victims. Keep it implied and cartoon.
- Lecturing during the story. Science waits for the calm segment at the end.

## Comment prompts that work
- Binary choice: "WOULD YOU ANSWER? YES / NO"
- Counting: "HOW MANY CLUES DID YOU CATCH?"
- Prediction: "Which rule breaks first?" (community post poll)
- Personal: "Check your CO alarm. Reply when you did." (safety + engagement)

## Age and platform notes
- Core 13–24 on mobile: text must be readable at phone size, captions burned in for key words, Shorts as the discovery funnel.
- Set the videos as **not made for kids** (teen horror themes); keep them advertiser-friendly (no gore, no swearing).
- Secondary 25–40 viewers stay for the real-science segment. Keep it accurate (`Research System/Fact-Checking & Accuracy Systems/`).

## Validate with data
Every 2 weeks read the top 50 comments per Part and tag them: *irony / clue / debate / fear / science / sequel request*. The most frequent tag tells you what to amplify next series.
