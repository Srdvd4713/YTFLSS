## 🔬 1. OpenCreator
**Stage:** Full Pipeline (Scripts → Images → Voiceover → Translation → Editing)  
**Folder:** `Script Frameworks` / `Visual Storytelling` / `Voiceover & Narration` / `Editing & Packaging`  
**Repo:** https://github.com/krillinai/OpenCreator

### What It Does
Give Codex (or similar LLMs) a creator workspace to:
- Write full documentary scripts
- Generate images for scenes
- Create voiceover audio
- Translate videos into multiple languages
- Edit content into a final product

### Use in Your Workflow
- Use as your **central production hub** — research in, finished video out
- Plug into your **Script Frameworks** folder as your primary scripting tool
- Feed translated outputs to reach **international documentary audiences**

### Key Notes
- Requires Codex/LLM API access
- Best paired with your script prompt libraries

## 🔍 2. Social Media Research Skills
**Stage:** Research, Competitor Analysis, Hook Extraction  
**Folder:** `Research System` / `Niche & Topic Selection`  
**Repo:** https://github.com/ScrapeCreators

### What It Does
Use Claude Code or Codex to:
- Research viral posts across platforms
- Analyze competitor channels
- Mine comment sections for audience pain points
- Extract hooks from video transcripts

### Use in Your Workflow
- **Weekly competitor breakdown** — what's going viral in your doc niche
- **Hook mining** from comment sections to fuel your curiosity gap scripts
- Feed results into your `Research Prompt Libraries` folder

### Key Notes
- Works with Claude Code or Codex
- Outputs structured data you can feed into scripting tools

## 📚 3. Local Deep Research
**Stage:** Topic Research & Report Generation  
**Folder:** `Research System` / `Source Gathering Workflow`  
**Repo:** https://github.com/LearningCircuit/local-deep-research

### What It Does
Turn any topic into a detailed research report using:
- Web searches (live data)
- Your own local documents
- Local OR cloud AI models (runs offline)

### Use in Your Workflow
- **Pre-production research** — drop a documentary topic in, get a sourced report out
- Supports your `Fact-Checking & Accuracy Systems` workflow
- Run locally = no API costs for deep research sessions

### Key Notes
- Can run fully offline with local models (Ollama, LM Studio, etc.)
- Ideal for evergreen documentary topics that need deep sourcing

## 🎨 4. FLUX.2 (Black Forest Labs)
**Stage:** Image Generation for Thumbnails & Visuals  
**Folder:** `Thumbnails & Titles` / `Visual Storytelling`  
**Repo:** https://github.com/black-forest-labs/flux

### What It Does
Generate and edit high-quality images for:
- Documentary thumbnails
- Social post visuals
- Scene illustration / B-roll substitutes
- Marketing campaign assets

### Use in Your Workflow
- **Thumbnail creation pipeline** — prompt → FLUX.2 → thumbnail
- Generate custom scene art when stock footage isn't available
- Klein 4B models have Apache 2.0 license = commercial use safe

### Key Notes
- Apache-licensed weights = commercially usable
- Klein 4B is the lightweight, locally runnable variant
- Pair with your `Curiosity-Driven Thumbnails` folder for prompts

## 🎙️ 5. Kokoro TTS
**Stage:** AI Voiceover Generation  
**Folder:** `Voiceover & Narration`  
**Repo:** https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro

### What It Does
Convert written scripts into natural-sounding AI voiceovers:
- Compact open-weight model (runs locally)
- Multiple voice styles and tones
- Fast generation speed

### Use in Your Workflow
- **Primary narration engine** for faceless documentaries
- Test multiple voice tones against your script's emotional arc
- Zero per-minute API costs (runs local)

### Key Notes
- Open-weight = run on your own hardware
- Pair with `Pacing & Emotion Control` notes to match voice to narrative mood
- Compare against ElevenLabs for quality benchmarking

## 🎵 6. ACE-Step 1.5
**Stage:** Music & Background Audio Generation  
**Folder:** `Editing & Packaging` / `Music & Sound Design Systems`  
**Repo:** https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step

### What It Does
Generate original music from text prompts:
- Create documentary-style instrumentals
- Regenerate specific audio sections
- Runs locally on supported hardware

### Use in Your Workflow
- **Custom score generation** — no royalty issues, no licensing fees
- Prompt: `"cinematic orchestral documentary, tense investigation, minor key"`
- Regenerate just the climax section without re-generating the whole track

### Key Notes
- Local execution = one-time setup, unlimited generations
- Store your best music prompts in `Music & Sound Design Systems`

## ✂️ 8. AI YouTube Shorts Generator
**Stage:** Long → Short Video Conversion  
**Folder:** `Editing & Packaging` / `Growth Engine`  
**Repo:** https://github.com/Anil-matcha/AI-Youtube-Shorts-Generator

### What It Does
Convert long YouTube videos into vertical Shorts:
- AI-selected highlights (best moments)
- Auto-transcription
- Automatic vertical crop/reframe

### Use in Your Workflow
- **Every full documentary → 3–5 Shorts automatically**
- Feeds your Shorts upload cadence without extra scripting
- Use transcript output for SEO descriptions and chapter markers

### Key Notes
- Works on existing YouTube URLs
- Pair with `Analytics That Matter` to track which Shorts drive full-doc views

## 📣 10. Social Media Agent (LangChain)
**Stage:** Distribution & Social Posting  
**Folder:** `Growth Engine` / `Automation & Systems`  
**Repo:** https://github.com/langchain-ai/social-media-agent

### What It Does
Turn a URL into ready-to-post social content:
- Auto-drafts X (Twitter) and LinkedIn posts

- Schedule through an AI agent workflow

### Use in Your Workflow
- **Post every documentary automatically** to X + LinkedIn on upload day
- Repurpose doc hook as teaser content to drive YouTube clicks
- Part of your `Full Faceless Doc Workflow` SOP

### Key Notes
- LangChain-native = easily extended with custom logic
- Store your best post templates in `Automation & Systems\Prompt Libraries`
