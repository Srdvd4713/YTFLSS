# Tension & Payoff Timing Map
*When to build pressure and when to release it, for a single Part and for a 6-Part series.*

## Inside one Part (3–8 min)
Use a **saw-tooth**: tension climbs, a small release, then climbs higher. Never two releases in a row.

| % of runtime | Beat | Music mood | Example (S01 P1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–8% | Hook: threat shown | `dread` | Figures at the treeline |
| 8–20% | Rules / context | `rules` | Noah writes the 5 rules |
| 20–35% | First test, small release | `tension` → `cabin` | Headache eases by the woodpile |
| 35–55% | Escalation #1 | `tension` | Smoke alarm chirps, batteries pulled |
| 55–60% | False calm | `cabin` | June asleep, hum steady |
| 60–85% | Escalation #2 (biggest) | `dread` | The figures walk closer |
| 85–95% | Cliffhanger | `sting` → `silence` | "They know our names." |
| 95–100% | End card | `drone` | WOULD YOU ANSWER? |

**Rule of thumb:** a release every 60–90 s, each release shorter than the last.

## Across the 6-Part series
| Part | Series tension | Payoff delivered | Payoff withheld |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setup, 3/10 → 6/10 | Rules + the threat | What the Tall Ones are |
| 2 | 6 → 7 | Clues #9–12 | Why they know the names |
| 3 | 7 → 8 | Biscuit recovers outside | Why outside helps |
| 4 | 8 → 9 | June feels better with the window open | Is it really Grandpa? |
| 5 | 9 → **10 (twist)** | All 12 clues replay, HUD rewrite | Do they get out? |
| 6 | 10 → release | Door opens, rescue, real science | Sequel sting (new tension) |

## Payoff rules
- **Plant ≥ 2 Parts before payoff** for the main twist (clues in P1–P2, payoff in P5).
- **Every plant pays off.** If a clue never pays off, cut it. Keep the clue table in the series framework current.
- **Payoff must be visual first, verbal second.** Show the headlamp on the rescuer, then say it.
- **Replays are earned.** In the twist Part, replay each clue in ≤ 2 s with the original frame plus the "real" label.
- **The release after the twist must teach.** Part 6's real-science segment is the documentary payoff: calm register, sources on screen.

## Pacing numbers for the render engine
- Average shot: 3–5 s. Max static shot: 6 s (add camera push or snow).
- Rule card on screen: 2.5–3.5 s.
- Silence before a scare: 0.8–1.5 s (`music: silence`).
- VO per shot: 1–2 sentences (narration rules in PROMPT LIBRARY).

## Script review questions
1. Where is the biggest moment? Is it in the last 15%?
2. Is there any stretch > 90 s with no release or no escalation?
3. Is every release smaller than the next escalation?
4. Does the final line open a question the next Part answers?
