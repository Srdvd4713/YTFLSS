# Hook Structures Playbook (Curiosity Gaps)
*The first 30 seconds of every Part. Built from the S01 hook pattern (PROMPT LIBRARY → Script) and the competitor research (Final Sight, Beanie Survives, Ifverse).*

## The job of the hook
In 0–30 s the viewer must (1) know the situation, (2) hold an open question they can't close without watching, and (3) feel the narrator is missing something. If the first 30 s retention in YouTube Studio is under ~70%, the hook is the first thing to rewrite.

## 0–30 s structure (every Part)
| Time | Beat | S01 example |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Cold line**: event + number + wrongness | "On the first night of the Quiet, two of them stood at the edge of the trees." |
| 3–8 s | **Visual proof**: show the threat, never explain it | `window_pov`: two white single eyes at the treeline |
| 8–15 s | **Rule flash**: the rule that seems to protect them | Rule card: *4. NEVER ANSWER THE TALL ONES.* |
| 15–25 s | **Irony plant**: one clue the viewer can see and Noah can't | HUD shows `AIR INSIDE: SAFE ✓` while the headache meter rises |
| 25–30 s | **Promise**: what this Part will answer | "By Day 3, they knew our names." |

## 7 hook templates
1. **Count + wrongness:** "On the [Nth] night of [EVENT], [N] of them [ACTION]."
2. **Rule + exception:** "There were five rules. We followed every one. That was the mistake." (twist promise without spoiler)
3. **Last normal moment:** "The last thing Grandpa said was *[line]*. Then the hum started."
4. **Body clue:** "My headache only stopped by the woodpile. I thought that meant the Quiet was inside." (viewer-solvable)
5. **Recap-in-one (Parts 2–6):** "Day 3. Fuel: four bars. Tall Ones seen: six. They know our names now."
6. **Question to the viewer:** "Would you open the door if your grandpa's voice was on the other side?"
7. **Science reversal (explainer shorts):** "Your headache going away outside is not a good sign."

## Curiosity-gap rules
- Open **one** big question per Part plus one micro-question every 45–60 s (see `Script Frameworks/Cliffhanger & Retention Techniques/`).
- Never answer the big question in the same Part, except Part 5 (twist) and Part 6 (resolution).
- The gap must be **specific**: "What do the lights want?" beats "Something is out there."
- Never lie to the viewer. Noah can be wrong; the visuals must stay truthful (every clue is fair play).
- Title, thumbnail and first line must open the **same** gap (thumbnail "DON'T ANSWER." → first line about the figures → rule 4 card by 15 s).

## Recap hooks for Parts 2–6
Keep recaps ≤ 12 s and frame them as the HUD/survival log, not "previously on". Use one shot from the last Part's cliffhanger, then a new image within 15 s.

## Checklist before locking a script
- [ ] First line ≤ 15 words, contains a number or a time marker
- [ ] Threat visible on screen by 5 s
- [ ] A rule card by 15 s
- [ ] One fair clue in the first 30 s
- [ ] The title's promise is addressed in the first 30 s
- [ ] No logo sting, no "welcome back", no subscribe ask before 60 s
