# S01 — THE QUIET AT KESTREL LAKE
*Series framework built on the Wall at Ashford formula (Adaptation Guide). Niche: serialized survival + rules + real-science twist.*

## A. Series concept

**Setting & world** — Grandpa Walt's log cabin on frozen Kestrel Lake, northern Minnesota, January. A blizzard knocked out power, phones and roads. Grandpa runs an old gas generator in the shed that's attached to the cabin. The nearest town is 40 miles away.

**Unreliable narrator** — **Noah, 15.** He's responsible to a fault, plays it deadpan, and has been "the man of the house" since Mom died. He makes rules because rules feel like control. He narrates everything as a survival log.

**What he believes (false reality)** — Something called **"the Quiet"** has taken all the sound out of the world: no radio, no planes, no phones. Grandpa's last note says *"Something is in the air."* So the danger is **outside**. At night, tall figures with one glowing white eye (the **Tall Ones**) stand at the treeline, call their names in stolen voices, and try to get in.

**What's actually happening (twist)** — The world is fine; it's a rural blackout. The generator's exhaust is leaking into the cabin, and they are slowly being poisoned with **carbon monoxide**. Grandpa felt the symptoms, went for help, and his note actually said *"keep the windows OPEN"* (the word got smudged by June's cocoa). The Tall Ones are a **search-and-rescue team** in pairs with headlamps and snowshoes, led by Grandpa. Every rule Noah wrote keeps the poison in and the rescuers out.

**Emotional core** — Noah and June (8), plus old beagle Biscuit. Noah would do anything to protect June, and everything he does to protect her is hurting her.

### The 5 Rules (3D cards)
1. **KEEP THE HUM GOING.** — the generator, which is the poison source
2. **KEEP EVERY WINDOW SHUT.** — this traps the CO
3. **NEVER GO PAST THE WOODPILE.** — the one place with fresh air, and where the rescuers' tracks are
4. **NEVER ANSWER THE TALL ONES.** — they're the rescuers
5. **NEVER OPEN THE DOOR.** — ***broken in Part 6***, and breaking it saves them

**Sequel hook** — Black screen, a generator hum fades in. New location, *"THE HUM UNDER DRY CREEK"*: a family sheltering in a basement after a Louisiana hurricane, running a generator by the stairwell. A new rules card flips: **1. KEEP THE HUM GOING.**

Each end card asks one question to drive comments: P1 *WOULD YOU ANSWER? YES / NO* · P2 *WHAT'S THE SKY SOUND?* · P3 *WHY DID BISCUIT WAKE UP?* · P4 *OPEN OR SHUT?* · P5 *HOW MANY CLUES DID YOU CATCH?* · P6 *CHECK YOUR CO ALARM.*

## B. Thumbnail formula
- **Palette:** midnight navy `#0b1426` → `#1c2c4f`, snow `#eef3fa`, warning amber `#ffc23d`, alarm red `#e5383b`. Max 3 dominant colours (impeccable rule).
- **Text:** 1–2 words, imperative, ending in a period ("DON'T ANSWER." / "KEEP IT SHUT." / "WRONG RULE."). Line 1 white, line 2 amber, heavy condensed font (Oswald 700). Top-left, ≥ 12% of frame height.
- **Composition:** foreground left-third is Noah (black round head, white oval eyes wide, red hoodie) facing the camera, back to the window. Background right is a frosted window with two tall silhouettes at the treeline, each with ONE white glowing light. Small "DAY 1" HUD chip bottom-right.
- **Emotional trigger:** dread plus dramatic irony. The viewer sees what Noah doesn't.
- **FLUX.2 prompt template** (art only, text is composited after so the brand font stays exact):
  > `flat 2D vector illustration, YouTube thumbnail, 16:9. A stickman boy with a solid black round head and big white oval eyes, wearing a red hoodie, [POSE/EXPRESSION], inside a dark log cabin at night. Behind him a frosted window shows a snowy pine treeline where two very tall dark figures stand, each with a single glowing white light for an eye. Deep navy and warm amber palette, strong rim light, clean bold shapes, high contrast, empty space top-left for text, no text, no watermark.`
