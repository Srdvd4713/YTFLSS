# YouTube SEO Checklist
*Search is a secondary traffic source for story channels (browse and suggested matter more), but the real-science angle gives every series search value: people search the hazard. This checklist runs on every upload.*

## Keyword research (per series, 15 min)
1. YouTube search autocomplete: type the hazard ("carbon monoxide", "generator") + "story", "symptoms", "why", "what happens".
2. Google Trends: compare 3–4 phrasings, pick the most searched.
3. `yt-dlp` search pull (PROMPT LIBRARY → Research) to see which phrasings carry views.
4. Pick: **1 primary keyword** for the series (e.g. "carbon monoxide story") + 3–5 secondary ("generator safety", "survival story animated", "snowed in cabin").

## Per-upload checklist
- [ ] **Title:** hook first; include a searchable word naturally if it doesn't hurt the hook (story Parts can skip; explainers must include it).
- [ ] **Description, first 2 lines:** hook + primary keyword in a natural sentence. These show in search.
- [ ] **Description body:** series context, Part links, playlist link, real-science note with sources, 3 hashtags.
- [ ] **Chapters:** timestamps starting at 0:00 (≥ 3 chapters, ≥ 10 s each). Name chapters with story beats, and "The real science" for P6.
- [ ] **Captions:** upload `output/partN.srt` (accurate transcript = indexed text).
- [ ] **Tags:** low weight; add series name, primary keyword and common misspellings only.
- [ ] **Playlist:** add to the series playlist (ordered Parts 1–7) and a "Real Science" playlist for explainers.
- [ ] **Thumbnail file name:** `quiet-kestrel-lake-part1.jpg` (harmless, tidy).
- [ ] **Language + caption language** set correctly (EN, ES, PT channels).
- [ ] **Audience:** not made for kids. Category: Film & Animation (story) or Education (explainers).

## Search-first formats (where SEO matters most)
- **Explainer Shorts and the Part 6 science segment:** title like the search ("Why Generators Cause Carbon Monoxide Poisoning").
- **Full-series movie cut:** "The Quiet at Kestrel Lake (Full Story) | Animated Survival Story".
- **Clue compilation:** brand search for the series name.
