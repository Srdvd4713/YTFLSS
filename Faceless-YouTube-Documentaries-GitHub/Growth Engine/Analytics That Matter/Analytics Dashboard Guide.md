# Analytics That Matter
*The few YouTube Studio numbers that decide what to change, and when to look at them. Log them in `EPISODE TRACKER.md` (Views column + session log).*

## The 6 metrics
| Metric | Where in Studio | What it tells you | Action if weak |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Impressions CTR** | Reach | Packaging (thumbnail + title) | A/B test (`Thumbnails & Titles/A_B Testing Systems/`) |
| **30-second retention** | Engagement → audience retention, "intro" | Hook matches the promise | Rewrite hook (`Script Frameworks/Hook Structures (Curiosity Gaps)/`) |
| **Average view %** | Engagement | Pacing and payoff | Tension map (`Script Frameworks/Tension & Payoff Timing/`) |
| **End-screen CTR / next-Part views** | Engagement → end screen | Cliffhanger strength | Cliffhanger playbook |
| **Returning vs new viewers** | Audience | Series loyalty | Stronger recaps, playlists, community posts |
| **Shorts → long-form** | Shorts "related video" link + audience | Is the funnel working? | Better "Part X is on the channel" endings, link the related video |

## Check cadence
| When | Look at | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 2 h after upload | CTR vs channel average, real-time views | Swap thumbnail if far below and no test is running |
| 48 h | 30-s retention, avg view %, retention spikes/drops | Notes for the next Part's script |
| 7 days | Traffic sources, end-screen CTR, subs gained per video | Which Part/Short drives growth |
| End of series | All Parts side by side | Update series framework + PROMPT LIBRARY with what worked |

## Benchmarks (our own, not the internet's)
Generic "good CTR" numbers vary by niche and traffic source. Use the **channel's own median** after 5 uploads as the baseline; judge each new video against it, comparing the same traffic source.

## Traps
- Don't judge a video in its first hour. Early numbers come from subscribers.
- High CTR + low retention = the packaging overpromised.
- Views alone don't count toward YPP: watch hours (long-form) and Shorts views are tracked separately.
- Comments: read them weekly and tag them (see `Niche & Topic Selection/Audience Psychology (Curiosity + Emotion)/`).
