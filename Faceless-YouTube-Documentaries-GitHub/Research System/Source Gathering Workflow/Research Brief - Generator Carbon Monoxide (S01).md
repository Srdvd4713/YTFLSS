# Research Brief — Blackout Generators & Carbon Monoxide

**Prompt used (Phase 5 step 1):** "Produce a detailed research report on carbon monoxide poisoning from portable generators during power outages. Include: verified facts, historical timeline, key figures, turning points, conflicting accounts, and shocking lesser-known details. Format as a documentary research brief."

## Verified facts
- CO is colourless and odourless. Exposure ranges from headache and fatigue up to coma and death. ([CDC MMWR](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm5428a2.htm))
- Symptoms are nonspecific and flu-like: headache, fatigue, dizziness, **confusion**, nausea, vomiting, then collapse and coma. 15–49% of survivors have lasting neurocognitive problems. ([FL Health / CDC NCEH](https://www.floridahealth.gov/Environmental-Health/carbon-monoxide/_documents/epa-presentation030410.pdf))
- The CPSC estimates **~85 U.S. deaths per year** from portable-generator CO (2011–2021). **81% happen at home.** ([CPSC 2022](https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2022/CPSC-Releases-New-Report-on-Carbon-Monoxide-CO-Fatalities-Urges-Generator-Safety-in-New-PSA))
- After the 2004 Florida hurricanes, misplaced generators (indoors, in garages, or outside near windows) caused nearly all CO exposures. ([CDC MMWR 54(28)](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm5428a2.htm))
- Generators placed outdoors but within 7 ft of the home still poisoned people. **The CPSC says 20 ft or more from windows, doors and vents.** ([CDC MMWR 55(09)](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm5509a4.htm))
- **Never** run one in a basement, garage or shed. A cracked window or door is **not** enough. ([PIRG summarising CPSC](https://pirg.org/newmexico/edfund/resources/portable-generators-kill-about-70-people-each-year-heres-how-to-protect-yourself-and-your-loved-ones/))

## Lesser-known details (story fuel)
- In one CDC cluster, 6 of 18 poisoned households had a CO detector, **and only one sounded.** Detectors that are missing, dead or disabled are a recurring factor. → Clue 5 (batteries pulled).
- Generator CO deaths reported to the CPSC doubled from 18 (2001) to 36 (2003) as generator ownership rose. ([CDC MMWR 54(28)](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm5428a2.htm))
- Confusion is a symptom, so victims often can't reason their way out. That's the core of the unreliable narrator.
