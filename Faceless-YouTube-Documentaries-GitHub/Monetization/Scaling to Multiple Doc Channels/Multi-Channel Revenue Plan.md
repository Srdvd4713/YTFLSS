# Multi-Channel Revenue Plan
*The business side of running more than one channel: when to open the next one, what it costs, and how revenue is tracked. The operational side (pipelines, schedules, files) lives in `Automation & Systems/Scaling Multiple Channels/`.*

## Gate to open a new channel
Open the next channel only if **all** are true:
1. Main channel has a repeatable weekly output for 4+ weeks without missing uploads.
2. The production pipeline runs from the SOP without manual fixes.
3. At least one series has a complete set of Parts to translate (no half series).
Alternative: YouTube's multi-language audio tracks on the main channel (if available to the channel) can test demand before opening a separate channel.

## Unit economics per Part
- Cost: hours × your hourly value + any paid review (translation, listen-through).
- Revenue: lifetime RPM × views (use the channel's own average after 3 months).
- A translated Part reuses ~80% of the work. If its 90-day revenue covers the translation cost, keep going.

## Risks
- **Reused content:** translated versions of your own original videos are fine; never upload the same video twice on different channels in the same language.
- **Spreading too thin:** a weak second channel hurts less than a missed upload on the main channel. Main channel first.
- **Account security:** each channel under a Brand Account with 2-Step Verification; keep recovery info current.
