# Affiliate Offers Plan
*Step 2 of the monetization roadmap. Affiliate links fit naturally after the real-science segment: the viewer just learned why a CO alarm matters. Only recommend products that solve the exact hazard shown.*

## Offer map by series hazard
| Series | Hazard | Product categories (examples) | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| S01 Kestrel Lake | Generator CO in a blackout | Battery CO alarms, plug-in CO alarm with battery backup, generator with CO auto-shutoff, generator cord to reach 20+ ft, NOAA weather radio | P6 description, explainer Shorts, pinned comment |
| S02 Dry Creek | Hurricane generator CO | Same as S01 + transfer switch info (link to an electrician guide, not a product) | P6 |
| S03 (hypothermia idea) | Car blizzard survival | Car winter kit, emergency blankets, hand warmers | P6 |
| Channel-wide | Creators | Mic, tools used to make the channel (only if asked) | About page / FAQ |

## Programs
- **Amazon Associates:** easiest start. Check the current program rules (it requires qualifying sales within the first 180 days or the account closes; links must be disclosed). Apply after the first series is live so the channel has content to review.
- **Brand programs** (safety brands, outdoor gear) via affiliate networks like Impact or ShareASale, once there are views to show.

## Disclosure (required)
- Description, above the links: "Some links are affiliate links. If you buy, the channel earns a small commission at no extra cost to you."
- If a brand gives you product or money for a mention, tick **"includes paid promotion"** in Studio.

## Description block template
```
🔧 STAY SAFE (affiliate links)
• Battery CO alarm: [link]
• Generator with CO shut-off: [link]
• 25 ft heavy-duty extension cord (keeps it 20+ ft away): [link]
Some links are affiliate links; the channel earns a small commission at no cost to you.
```

## Rules
- Recommend product *types* in the VO, never brand names in the story.
- One to three links per video. More looks spammy and lowers trust.
- Never pick a product you can't stand behind. Safety products should carry recognised certifications (e.g. UL listing for CO alarms in the US).
- Track clicks per video in the affiliate dashboard; move the best link into the pinned comment.
