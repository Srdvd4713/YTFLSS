# Memberships & Community Plan
*Step 4 of the monetization roadmap. Memberships need YPP eligibility (check the current requirements in Studio → Earn). Community building starts on day 1, long before memberships unlock.*

## Before memberships: community habits (start now)
- **Pinned comment on every upload** = the end-card question.
- **Reply to the first 20 comments** within the first hour (heart the best clue-hunters).
- **Community posts** (when unlocked): polls between Parts ("Which rule breaks first?"), clue-hunt images, behind-the-scenes stills.
- **Name the fans:** shout out the first person who solves the twist in Part 7's description.

## Membership tiers (draft)
| Tier | Price (suggested) | Perks |
|---|---|---|
| **Survivor** | $1.99/mo | Loyalty badge, custom emojis (Noah's eyes, rule card, headlamp), members-only community posts |
| **Search Party** | $4.99/mo | + Twist Part (P5) 24 h early, the "all 12 clues annotated" cut, name in the end credits of the next series |
| **Rule Maker** | $9.99/mo | + Vote on the next series' setting and hazard, monthly behind-the-scenes (script drafts, rejected thumbnails) |

Keep perks cheap to deliver: they reuse assets we already make (annotated cut = Part 7 with extra callouts).

## Emojis and badges
Design in the brand style (Oswald, navy/amber/red): headlamp eye, "SAFE ✓", rule card, Biscuit sleeping, generator. Export from the render engine as stills.

## Moderation
- Add words that spoil the twist to the blocked-words list during the first days of a series (e.g. "carbon monoxide", "CO") and remove them after Part 5 is out.
- Hold links for review. Turn on "hold potentially inappropriate comments".
- Keep it friendly for the 13–17 part of the audience.

## Metrics
Members count, churn per month, early-access views, poll participation. Review monthly with the Revenue tab.
