# AdSense Optimization Guide
*Step 1 of the monetization roadmap (CHANNEL BIBLE). Goal: reach the YouTube Partner Program, then keep videos advertiser-friendly and mid-roll-ready. Check current YPP thresholds in YouTube's help centre before planning around them.*

## Getting into YPP
- **Ad revenue tier (as of the last check):** 1,000 subscribers + either 4,000 public long-form watch hours in the last 12 months **or** 10M public Shorts views in the last 90 days. Shorts views do not count toward watch hours.
- Requirements: 2-Step Verification, no active Community Guidelines strikes, an AdSense account linked in Studio.
- Our path: long-form Parts are the watch-hour engine; the 25-min full-series cut adds hours fast; Shorts drive subscribers.

## Advertiser-friendly rules for this niche
| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Implied danger, cartoon style | Gore, blood, realistic injury |
| "Won't wake up", "the Quiet" | "Dead", "killed", "murder" in titles/thumbnails |
| Real safety facts with sources | Graphic description of real deaths |
| Calm science segment | Profanity, especially in the first 30 s |
If a video gets yellow-iconed (limited ads), request human review only if it's clearly compliant; otherwise fix and re-upload is rarely worth it. Learn the pattern instead.

## Mid-rolls
- Available on videos **8 min or longer**. Most Parts are 3–8 min, so:
  - Make **Part 6** (story + science) and the **full-series cut** ≥ 8 min.
  - Let YouTube place mid-rolls automatically, then **move them to natural breaks** (after a cliffhanger beat, black-screen transitions), never during a reveal.
- Don't stretch weak Parts to hit 8 min. Retention matters more.

## RPM levers
- Audience geography: US/UK/CA/AU viewers pay more; the 3–5 pm US posting slot targets them.
- Topic: safety/health/education themes attract better advertisers than pure horror. Keep the real-science segment in every series.
- Watch time per viewer: series playlists and the full cut.
- Q4 (Oct–Dec) pays the most: time a strong series release then.
