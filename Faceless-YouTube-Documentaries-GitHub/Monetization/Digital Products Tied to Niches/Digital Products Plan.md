# Digital Products Plan
*Step 3 of the monetization roadmap. Products are built from assets the series already produces (rule cards, clue tables, research briefs), so each costs a few hours, not weeks.*

## Product line
| Product | Built from | Format | Price idea | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Detective Kit: [Series]** | 12-clue table, rule cards, note prop, HUD, map | Printable PDF (10–15 pages) + phone wallpapers | $3–5 | Core fans 13–24 |
| **Family Storm-Readiness Rules** | Real-science segment + research brief | Printable card pack (fridge card, kid version, checklist) | $5–9 | Parents 25–40 |
| **Series art pack** | Thumbnail art, scene stills | Wallpapers (phone/desktop) | $2 or free lead magnet | Fans |
| **Educator pack** | Real-science segment + quiz | PDF lesson + video link | $10–15 | Teachers (safety units) |
| **Bundle per series** | All of the above | ZIP | $12 | Superfans |

## Where to sell
- Gumroad, Payhip or Ko-fi shop (simple, handles VAT on digital goods in many regions). Check fees and tax handling for your country.
- YouTube Shopping (product shelf) once eligible.
- Link in the description and in a pinned comment of Part 7 and Part 6.

## Build workflow (per series, ~4 h)
1. Export rule cards, note and HUD stills from the render engine (`node render.js --part N --still T`).
2. Lay out pages in Penpot (`Thumbnails & Titles/Curiosity-Driven Thumbnails/Penpot Design Tool.md`) using brand tokens.
3. Safety content: copy facts **only** from the fact-checked claims table; add "Source:" lines; add "This is not medical advice. In an emergency call 911 / your local emergency number."
4. Export PDF, test print in black and white, test on a phone.
5. Upload, add the link block to the descriptions.

## Free lead magnet
Give the **Detective Kit for Part 1–2 clues** free (email capture via the store). Announce the paid full kit after Part 5. Build an email list. It's the asset YouTube can't take away.

## Rules
- Don't spoil the twist on the product page before Part 5 is out.
- Keep the kid-facing products simple and safe; no horror imagery on the family/educator packs.
