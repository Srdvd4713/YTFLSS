# CHANNEL BIBLE

## Channel name options
1. **Survival Log** — matches the HUD ("● SURVIVAL LOG"); every series is a log of days.
2. **Rules of the Quiet** — leans into the rules-horror packaging (Final Sight / Bradikinin formula).
3. **The Wrong Rule** — sells the twist promise of every series: the rules were the danger.

*Recommendation: **Survival Log**. It survives beyond Series 1 and reads cleanly as a handle (@SurvivalLogTales). Check availability before committing.*

## Niche definition
Animated **serialized survival stories** (6 parts, day-counted) told by an unreliable narrator who follows **rules that turn out to be wrong**. Every twist is a **real, documentable hazard or historical event** (CO poisoning, hypothermia "paradoxical undressing", flash-flood myths, etc.). Each series ends with a short "the real science" explainer. That's the documentary spine, and it's what makes the twist land.

## Target audience
- **Core:** 13–24, watches Ifverse / Final Sight / Beanie Survives, YouTube on mobile, often "last video before bed" (that exact phrase shows up in Final Sight's top comments).
- **Secondary:** 25–40 true-crime and survival-doc viewers. They come for the real-science reveal and drive higher-CPM watch time.
- **Motivations:** dread with a safe landing, solving the puzzle before the reveal (clue-hunting = rewatches), and "would you have survived?" comment debates.

## Brand voice
- Narrator: first-person teen, **deadpan and understated**. Short sentences. He reports horror like a weather log.
- Never winks at the audience. The irony lives in the visuals and the HUD, never in his lines.
- One-line hooks: "On the first night of the Quiet, two of them stood at the edge of the trees."
- Every episode ends with a **binary question** for the comments.
- Real-science segments switch to a calm, factual "documentary" register and cite sources on screen.

## Upload schedule
- **Long-form (16:9, 3–8 min):** daily during a series (Day 1–6 = Parts 1–6, Day 7 = clue compilation).
- **Shorts (9:16):** 3 per long-form episode, posted the same day and the following 2 days, each ending "Part X is on the channel."
- Post time: 3–5 pm in the audience's main time zone (testing window). Keep the same slot every day.

## Monetization roadmap
1. **AdSense** (YPP at 1K subs + 4K watch hours, or 10M Shorts views). Long-form drives it; Part 6 and the science explainers are advertiser-safe.
2. **Affiliate:** CO alarms, generator safety kits, emergency radios, winter survival gear. These link naturally from the real-science segments (Amazon Associates).
3. **Digital products:** printable "Family Storm-Readiness Rules" card pack, plus the series' rules/clue PDFs as a "detective kit".
4. **Memberships:** early access to Part 5 (the twist), a members-only "all 12 clues annotated" cut, vote on Series 3's setting.
5. **Brand ad pipeline:** animated explainers for brands at $500–$5K. The channel is the portfolio (Stickman SOP).
