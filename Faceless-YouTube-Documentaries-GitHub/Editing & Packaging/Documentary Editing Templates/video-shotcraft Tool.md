# video-shotcraft (13K ?)
**GitHub:** https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft
**Stage:** Promo & Preview Video Rendering

## What It Does
1. Captures screenshots of your real pages / content
2. Selects from 150+ shot recipes
3. Renders a polished promo video via Remotion

## Use for Documentary Channel
- Auto-generate episode trailers from your video page
- Create social promo clips for X, LinkedIn, Instagram
- Build channel trailer from your top video thumbnails
- Generate Patreon membership pitch video

## 150 Shot Recipe Categories (Selected)
| Recipe Type | Best For |
|-------------|----------|
| Hero Zoom-In | Documentary opener / reveal |
| Split Reveal | Before/after, contrast storytelling |
| Text Slam | Shocking stat or quote reveal |
| Device Mockup | Showing research sources |
| Pan Across | Timeline or map visuals |
| Focus Pull | Highlighting key detail |
| Glitch Transition | Crime / conspiracy tone |

## Workflow
1. Provide URL of your YouTube page or thumbnail collection
2. Pick 3–5 shot recipes that match your doc's tone
3. Render ? MP4 output ready to post

## Notes
- Built on Remotion — outputs clean, reusable components
- Use the same 3–5 recipe set for every episode for consistent promo style
