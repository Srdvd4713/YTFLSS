# ACE-Step 1.5
**GitHub:** https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step
**Stage:** Background Music + Score Generation
**Run:** Local (supported hardware)

## What It Does
Generate original royalty-free music from text prompts:
- Full instrumentals
- Section-by-section generation
- Regenerate specific parts without losing the rest

## Documentary Music Prompt Templates

### Investigation / Crime
> "cinematic orchestral, tense investigation, minor key, slow build, no lyrics, documentary score"

### History / Epic
> "epic orchestral, sweeping strings, historical documentary, emotional, triumphant ending"

### Mystery / Conspiracy
> "dark ambient, subtle tension, drone bass, mysterious, cinematic, no melody"

### Science / Nature
> "ambient electronic, curious, wonder, light percussion, uplifting, nature documentary"

### Intro / Outro
> "dramatic opening sting, 8 seconds, orchestral hit, cinematic documentary"

## Workflow
1. Write your music prompt
2. Generate full track
3. Regenerate climax section if needed
4. Export ? layer in editing software
